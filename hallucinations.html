Asking the Right Question: An Empirical Evaluation of a Decision Language Model for Detecting Vague Requirements

A vibe-coded paper, shared for illustration only. It is not a submission and has not been peer-reviewed. The experiments, the analysis and the text were produced with AI coding tools. The paper evaluates a 9-billion-parameter decision model on detecting vague weak words in requirements, using QuRE, ARTA and ReqEval.

Achieving Compliance with the EU AI Act

Which methods and tools do organisations use to comply with the EU AI Act? Based on 67 sources, the report finds that no single method is field-validated. The evidence favours combining compliance-by-design, LLM-assisted documentation with human legal review, and clear governance roles.
